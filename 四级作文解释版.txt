						Is technology making people lazy?
	owning to the pervasive use of digital devices,whether technology makes people lazy has intrigued the public.（由于数码设备的广泛使用，科技是否会让人变懒引起了大众的兴趣。）  As far as I am concerned my perspective(在我看来),the demerits(缺点) of digital devices are innumerable and manifold.（数码设备的缺点非常多） 
	In the first place,what must be prioritized is that(首先，必须被强调的第一点是) digital devices are now making people lazy.Order takeout(点外卖) is a case in point（点外卖就是一个很好的例子）.People just need to place the orders on their mobile phone and wait for the food to be arrived home.(人们只需在手机上下订单，等待食物送到家中即可。) 
	In the second place(第二),never can people ignore（忽视） the indispensability of（人们永远都不能忽视...[数码设备]的重要性）digital devices.  There are myriad reason for this.(有非常多的原因解释它)  The prime reason is that(主要原因是)some people use digital devices to be lazy and don't study.(有些人用数字设备来偷懒，不学习)  For example,students are hanging online classes with their mobile phones.(例如，学生用手机挂网课)
	All in all(总而言之),it is natural to reach the conclusion that(很自然的得到...[科技正在让人变懒]结论)technology is making people lazy.
